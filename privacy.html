Privacy Policy

IKnowGeography Uploader · Last updated 4 November 2026

Who uses this app

IKnowGeography Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the IknowGeography YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information from anyone else.

What data it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to:

  • upload and manage videos, thumbnails and captions on the IknowGeography channel;
  • read the channel's YouTube data and YouTube Analytics reports.

The app does not access Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data, and does not access any other person's YouTube account.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish videos to the IknowGeography channel and to review that channel's performance.
  • The Google sign-in token and analytics reports are stored only on the channel owner's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from reaching the channel; locally stored tokens can then be deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the IknowGeography YouTube channel.
