IKnowGeography Uploader

A private publishing tool for the IknowGeography YouTube channel.

What it does

IKnowGeography Uploader is a personal tool run by the owner of the IknowGeography channel on their own computer. It:

  • uploads finished geography explainer videos, thumbnails and captions to the IknowGeography YouTube channel;
  • sets each video's title, description, tags and scheduled publish time;
  • reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, click-through rate) to plan future videos.

It is not a public service. It only connects to the IknowGeography channel, after the channel owner signs in with Google and grants access.
